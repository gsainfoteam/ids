Add the `ids-skeleton-pulse` and `ids-skeleton-wave` keyframes with the `animate-skeleton-pulse` and
`animate-skeleton-wave` utilities. Pulse moves the background color from `--ids-color-muted` to
`--ids-color-muted-hover` and back over 2s. Wave slides the element's background image, sized to
half its width, from past the left edge to past the right edge in 1.2s and rests 0.8s; reverse its
direction for right-to-left text.
