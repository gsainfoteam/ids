Tabs adds `appearance="segmented"`: a `bg-neutral-subtle` track at `concentric-p-1` that is as tall
as a control, with the selected tab as a card in the new `bg-tabs-segment` component token
(`bg-neutral-subtlest` in light, `bg-neutral` in dark) with `shadow-xs`. `underline` and
`enclosed` draw the selected label in `text-primary` (the `colorScheme`'s `text-X`).
