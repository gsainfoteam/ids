Select, ChipField and Menu mark a chosen option with a `text-primary` check or dot and no
background: a selected option and a checked `Menu.CheckboxItem` or `Menu.RadioItem` keep the list's
surface, so the only filled row is the highlighted one. The highlight stays on the neutral ladder
(`bg-neutral-soft`, pressed `bg-neutral-soft-hover`) whether or not the option is chosen, and a Menu
item whose submenu is open takes the press step again.
