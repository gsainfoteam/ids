`@gsainfoteam/ids-css/fonts.css` ships the default fonts, Pretendard GOV (variable, dynamic
subset) and Geist Mono (variable), with their woff2 files and OFL licenses inside the package.
`@import '@gsainfoteam/ids-css/fonts.css';` after `@gsainfoteam/ids-css` and the app's bundler
emits the files. No CDN or font package is needed.
