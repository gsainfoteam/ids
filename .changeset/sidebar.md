Add `Sidebar`, the app's main menu: `Sidebar.Header`, `Group`, `Item`, `Footer` and `Trigger`, with the optional `Sidebar.Provider` and `Sidebar.Inset` for a trigger and page outside it. Items mark the current page with `aria-current="page"`, items nest to any depth (Accordions in the panel, nested Menus in the icon rail), and `variant` picks `sidebar`, `floating` or `inset`. `collapsible` folds it to an icon rail with tooltips or takes it off the canvas, `Mod+B` toggles it, and `persist` writes the state to a cookie that the server can pass back as `defaultCollapsed`. `Sidebar.Group` takes its items as JSX or as data: `items` (an `{ id, children }` tree) and a function that renders one row, with the children placed under it. With `onItemsChange` those items can be dragged before, after or into another item (with `@dnd-kit/core`) or picked up with Space and moved with the arrow keys; it receives the new array and the move (`item`, `from`, `to` with parent, ancestors, index and siblings), and `canMove` decides where an item may go. `Sidebar.Item colorScheme` tints its icon, and the menu fades at the edge that has more to scroll. Below 640px the sidebar opens in a Drawer sized for touch that closes when an item is pressed.
